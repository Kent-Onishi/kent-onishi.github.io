Kentaro Onishi is an aspiring political scientist from Japan, concerning the various governmental policy on elections and civic engagement as its outcome, from comparative perspective. In my upcoming doctoral program, I aim to cultivate comprehensive understanding of the basis of political science studies; I aim to take it further by utilizing the modern technologies to analyze numerical data of civic engagement across the world.

Civic engagement is a broad concept seen across academia, from polisci to psychology. Britannica describes that "broad set of practices and attitudes of involvement in social and political life that converge to increase the health of a democratic society" (Britannica).
